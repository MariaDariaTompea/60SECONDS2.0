============================================================
  SHIP HELM SPECIFICATIONS — 60Seconds 2.0
  (The steering wheel + railing area at the top of screen)
============================================================

  "Helm" = the ship's steering station.
  In nautical terms:
    - The WHEEL (roata) = the steering wheel
    - The HELM BOARD (bordul) = the wooden railing / dashboard
      where the wheel is mounted

GAME CANVAS:  1920 x 1080 px  (16:9)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ELEMENT 1 — HELM BOARD (the railing / dashboard bar)

  Max Width:   1920 px   (spans the full screen width)
  Max Height:  180 px

  This is the wooden railing / ship board that sits at the
  very top of the screen, ABOVE the decorative border.
  Draw it as a horizontal wooden plank / railing.

  FILE:  helm_board.png  (transparent PNG)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ELEMENT 2 — STEERING WHEEL (roata / manivela)

  Max Width:   240 px
  Max Height:  240 px

  This is the ship's steering wheel. It will be drawn as a
  SEPARATE element so the code can ROTATE it continuously
  (like the ocean waves — slow, rhythmic rotation).

  The wheel should be centered on its canvas so rotation
  looks correct (center of wheel = center of 240×240 canvas).

  FILE:  helm_wheel.png  (transparent PNG)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLACEMENT:

  The helm board sits at the very top of the screen (Y: 0).
  The wheel is positioned centered horizontally on the board,
  overlapping it slightly:

    Wheel center X:  ~960 px  (center of screen)
    Wheel center Y:  ~120 px  (overlapping bottom of board)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FILE FORMAT:   PNG with transparency (32-bit RGBA)
COLOR MODE:    sRGB
DPI:           72 (screen)
============================================================
