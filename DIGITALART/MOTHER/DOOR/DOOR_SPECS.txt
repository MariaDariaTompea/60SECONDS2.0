DOOR CANVAS SIZE (for drawing):

  Full canvas:  1920 x 1080 px

  You will draw TWO HALVES in this canvas:

  TOP DOOR (upper half):
    Area:  1920 x 580 px   (from Y=0 to Y=580)

  BOTTOM DOOR (lower half):
    Area:  1920 x 580 px   (from Y=500 to Y=1080)

  ⚠ NOTE: The top door extends 80px BELOW the midpoint (540)
    and the bottom door starts 40px ABOVE the midpoint (500).
    This creates a slight OVERLAP zone (500–580) so the top
    door sits slightly above the bottom door when they meet.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOW THE ANIMATION WORKS:

  1. Top door slides DOWN from above the screen → stops at center
  2. Bottom door slides UP from below the screen → stops at center
  3. They overlap for ~1 second (top door in front)
  4. Then they slide apart — top goes UP, bottom goes DOWN
  5. Behind them: the full menu is revealed
