\vspace{1cm}

This Project Collaboration and Revenue Sharing Agreement (the ``Agreement'') is entered into as of \textbf{September 1, 2026}, by and between:

\vspace{0.5cm}
\noindent \textbf{Project Manager:} Maria-Daria Tompea, Student at Babeș-Bolyai University. \\
\noindent \textbf{And the undersigned Participant} (the ``Participant'').

\section{1. PROJECT OVERVIEW AND MANAGEMENT}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, label={}]
    \item 1.1. The Project consists of the development, production, and distribution of a video game, currently titled \textbf{``Revamp of 60 seconds 2027''} (the ``Project'').
    \item 1.2. The Project is managed exclusively by Maria-Daria Tompea (the ``Manager'').
    \item 1.3. Team Composition: The Project team shall consist of a minimum of four (4) and a maximum of eight (8) participants.
    \item 1.4. Dissolution Clause: In the event the Project is abandoned by members such that fewer than four (4) active participants remain, the Project shall be officially disbanded.
\end{itemize}

\section{2. TIMELINE AND KEY MILESTONES}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, label={}]
    \item 2.1. Commencement: Tasks and official development shall begin on \textbf{September 1, 2026}.
    \item 2.2. First Demo: A functional project demo must be completed by \textbf{March 20, 2027}.
    \item 2.3. Public Production Release: The first public release (production version) is scheduled for \textbf{June 1, 2027}.
    \item 2.4. Distribution Platform: The Project shall be hosted on a platform or website that allows users a trial period of three (3) hours before requiring a purchase for the full version.
\end{itemize}

\section{3. REVENUE SHARING AND PAYMENT STRUCTURE}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, label={}]
    \item 3.1. Revenue Distribution Period: The sharing ratios for artists are valid for the first five (5) years post-launch. The Manager’s base percentage is fixed for the first three (3) years.
    \item 3.2. Performance-Based Tiers: Payment to participants is contingent upon total revenue gained by the Project (the ``Company Gains''), structured as follows:
    
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5cm, label={--}]
        \item \textbf{A. Revenue under 1,000 EUR:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Artists (max 4): 10\% each.
            \item Programmers: 15\% each.
        \end{itemize}
        \item \textbf{B. Revenue between 1,000 EUR and 5,000 EUR:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Artists (max 4): 8\% each.
            \item Programmers: 13\% each.
        \end{itemize}
        \item \textbf{C. Revenue above 5,000 EUR:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Artists (max 4): 6\% each.
            \item Programmers: 11\% each.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item 3.3. Manager’s Share: For the first three (3) years, the Manager shall receive 25\% of the total Company Gains.
    \item 3.4. Promotional Fund: All remaining percentages of revenue not allocated to Participants or the Manager’s base share shall be strictly allocated to promotional and marketing content.
    \item 3.5. Unallocated Slots: If the team consists of fewer than eight (8) members, the percentages that would have been allocated to the remaining slots must be redirected into the promotional fund.
    \item 3.6. Promotion Adjustment: After three (3) years, the Manager reserves the right to close or adjust the promotional budget allocation, provided all funds not distributed to team members continue to be spent on promotional activities.
    \item 3.7. Social Media Marketing Bonus: Any Participant who creates a social media account dedicated specifically to the Project for marketing purposes shall be entitled to an additional 3\% (three percentage points) added to their respective revenue share, provided they meet the following monthly criteria:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5cm, label={--}]
        \item Post at least ten (10) unique marketing videos per month.
        \item Each video must have a duration of at least 35 seconds and no more than 70 seconds (1 minute and 10 seconds).
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\section{4. PARTICIPANT OBLIGATIONS AND TERMINATION}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, label={}]
    \item 4.1. Task Deadlines: Starting September 1, 2026, tasks will be assigned with a strict two-week (14-day) deadline.
    \item 4.2. ``Two-Strike'' Policy: If a Participant fails to complete assigned work or refuses to collaborate effectively on two (2) separate occasions, they will be subject to immediate deselection from the Project.
    \item 4.3. Consequences of Removal: Any Participant deselected under Clause 4.2 forfeits all rights to future revenue sharing, intellectual property claims, and final project credits.
    \item 4.4. Collaboration Standard: Participants are expected to maintain professional communication and provide regular updates within the Project’s chosen communication channels.
    \item 4.5. GitHub Collaboration: All technical and artistic development shall be conducted within a shared GitHub repository. Each Participant is required to create a GitHub account and connect to the official Project repository. This system will be used to monitor the state of development, task completion, and individual contributions.
\end{itemize}

\section{5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND CONFIDENTIALITY}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, label={}]
    \item 5.1. Ownership: All assets, code, and materials created for the Project are the property of the Project entity managed by Maria-Daria Tompea.
    \item 5.2. Confidentiality: Participants agree not to disclose internal Project details, source code, or unreleased art assets to third parties without express written consent from the Manager.
\end{itemize}
