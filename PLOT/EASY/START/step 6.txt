ENDING: A New Dawn on Solace Cay
The waters finally settled, leaving behind a smooth, silent sea. Taking advantage of the calm, we hauled anchor and guided our ship safely into the shallow bay of Solace Cay.

We made it. We actually escaped the cursed sea.

We are starting over from scratch, with nothing but a few salvaged supplies and the tents we built. We miss our home terribly:the lives, the kingdom, and the people we left behind in The Tempestate , but this untainted island will do.

Here, on these quiet shores, we will build a new sanctuary. This is our territory now.

Summary of your Game’s Prologue / Early Act Loop:
Intro: Fleeing the seaport during the outbreak of the Sea Curse; boarding the ship with minimal supplies.

Step 2 (Scavenge Choice): Discovering the map leading to Solace Cay; deciding whether to risk landing.

Step 3 (Tool Choice): Sending a scout with a Map, Gun, or Bare Hands to secure the beachhead.

Step 4 (Survivor Choice): Selecting a crew member to expand the camp (or skipping until ready).

Step 5 (Construction Complete): The small village/camp is built while the stormy sea calms down.

Step 6 (Victory / Ending): Anchoring safely at Solace Cay and founding a new home.
