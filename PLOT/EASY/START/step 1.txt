Life on the coast of The Tempestate was once peaceful, but peace is a fragile thing when dark forces awaken.

The Sea Curse—an ancient, insidious magic spread like a plague across the realm. It shattered kingdoms, twisted the weather into violent storms, and corrupted every living creature it touched, turning peaceful people into mindless, aggressive shells of their former selves.

When the curse finally reached our shores, chaos broke out. Driven to the edge of the seaport with nowhere left to run, we had only seconds to react. We grabbed whatever supplies were within arm’s reach, boarded a vessel, and cast off into the unknown—hoping against hope that the meager resources we salvaged would hold out until our next scavenge.

Strangers thrown together on a battered ship, fleeing an apocalyptic blight. What could possibly go wrong?

Yet distance brought no safety. As we ventured deeper into the ocean, it became clear that the Sea Curse followed us. Monstrous waves battered our hull, while howling winds and torrential rain tried to drag us under. We were pinned in place, helpless against the sea, barely surviving the storm's brutal first wave.

We know dangerously little about this curse or what it wants. For now, all we can do is hold on... and try not to test its patience any further.
