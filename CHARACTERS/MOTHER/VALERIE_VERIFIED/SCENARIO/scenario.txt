================================================================================
THE UNLOCK SCENARIO: THE JESTER'S CONGREGATION (7-STEP EVENT)
================================================================================

[PAGE 1: THE FIRST ENCOUNTER]
The horizon bleeds an unnatural, bruised purple as the ship drifts through an 
unmapped stretch of dead water. The lookout rings the bell, pointing toward a 
sole solitary object bobbing in the waves: a rotting wooden chair floating 
perfectly upright upon the sea. Sitting upon it is a figure dressed in tattered, 
clownish crimson nun vestments, her face hidden behind a pitch-black veil. As the 
ship draws near, she doesn't cry out for help. She simply sits there, hands 
neatly folded in her lap, watching the hull approach with a wide, razor-toothed 
grin that gleams in the twilight. The crew shivers; the sea around her chair is 
thick, black, and completely devoid of waves.

[PAGE 2: THE SECOND ENCOUNTER & CONVERSATION]
Against the crew's better judgment, the ship draws close enough to speak. The 
figure tilts her head, her jagged red crown glinting as she speaks in a voice 
that sounds like grinding tectonic plates and rushing tide water. 
"Oh, look at you! A brave little box of kindling floating right into the 
slaughterhouse," she purrs, her grin widening. "Tell me, little sailors, did the 
world promise you golden shores? Did your gods tell you honesty brings good 
fortune? Let me let you in on a little secret... the only thing waiting for you 
at the end of your righteous path is the butcher's cleaver!" She laughs, a 
short, musical sound that makes the timbers of your ship groan. "I played their 
game. I bled for their altars. And the sea taught me the punchline."

[PAGE 3: THE DECISION (YES / NO)]
The strange nun leans forward, resting her chin in her hands, her unblinking veil 
staring directly into the captain's eyes. "I am bored of watching the tides turn 
empty shells. I want to see how your little tragedy ends. Let me speak to your 
tools, or keep rowing your wooden coffin into the dark. Do you care to play a 
game of wits with a dead woman?"
-> OPTION A: Yes. Listen to what she wants. (Proceed to Page 4)
-> OPTION B: No. Steer clear of this madness and row away. (Event Ends. Valerie 
             disappears into the fog; global sanity drops by 10).

[PAGE 4: THE FIRST TOLL (ITEM REQUIRED - NOT DESTROYED)]
The nun claps her hands together in delight. "Splendid! A audience! But a 
performer requires a stage setting. Show me how you document your pathetic 
little lives before the water claims them. Give me something to read, or 
something to cut through the boring prose of your existence."
To satisfy her curiosity without angering the sea, you must present an object of 
intellect or structure. (The selected item WILL NOT break).
-> REQUIREMENT: Provide [Map], [Journal], or [Axe].
   - If [Map]: She traces the lines with a long, clawed finger, mocking the borders.
   - If [Journal]: She laughs at the mundane entries of terrified men.
   - If [Axe]: She tests the edge against her pale skin, whispering of old purges.
(Proceed to Page 5)

[PAGE 5: THE SACRIFICIAL TOLL (ITEM REQUIRED - DESTROYED)]
Her smile turns sharp, hungry. The black water around her chair begins to rise, 
creeping up the sides of your hull like living ink. "Now, a token of true faith," 
she whispers. "The things that keep your bodies warm and your bellies full are 
the very things that blind you to the deep. Destroy your comfort, or let the sea 
swallow your steering wheel."
You must sacrifice an essential survival tool to appease the growing curse. 
(The selected item WILL BE permanently broken/consumed).
-> REQUIREMENT: Sacrifice [Lantern] OR [Medkit].
   - If [Lantern]: You smash the glass, throwing the flame into the black sea. It 
     burns blue under the water before dying. (Lantern destroyed).
   - If [Medkit]: You dump the medicine and bandages overboard; they instantly 
     dissolve into a foul, brackish sludge. (Medkit destroyed).
(Proceed to Page 6)

[PAGE 6: THE ABYSSAL MANIFESTO]
With the sacrifice accepted, the nun stands up from her floating chair. She 
walks across the surface of the water as if it were solid stone, her heavy 
skirts leaving trails of glowing, bioluminescent rot behind her. She steps over 
the railing of your ship, her presence instantly causing the lanterns to flicker 
and cast elongated, jester-like shadows across the deck. "The bargain is struck," 
she whispers, her unhinged jaw twitching. "The deep has tasted your desperation, 
and it finds the flavor... exquisite. I can anchor myself to your wooden ribs, 
or I can leave a drop of my blood in your stew to guide your path."

[PAGE 7: THE FINAL COMPACT (YES / NO)]
The entity stands before you, her jagged red crown dripping with cold brine. The 
ship's ledger lies open on the table. You must choose how to utilize this 
shattered entity.
-> OPTION A: Welcome her aboard as a permanent crew member.
   (UNLOCKS CHARACTER: Sister Valerie is added to your roster. She takes up a 
   crew slot. If you already own her, this entire event chain is permanently 
   disabled from future runs).
-> OPTION B: Refuse her residency, but demand her magic for the journey.
   (Valerie vanishes back into the sea with a manic shriek. The ship does not 
   unlock her, but instantly gains +40% Sea Curse, resets all current crew 
   members' health to full, but reduces everyone's sanity to 15%).
